01 / The approach

From market history
to a testable idea.

A useful result needs a clear path back to its inputs. Keep that path visible, from collection through review to offline simulation.

Start with the source

A history you can trace.

Collect completed candles with a read-only MT5 script. Record the provider, instrument, timeframe and verified broker timezone alongside the original export.

  • Completed candles only
  • Explicit timezone and daylight-saving review
  • Original file preserved with a SHA-256 checksum

Make the evidence reviewable

Confidence begins with checks.

Inspect timestamps, price relationships and gaps. Review source rights and dataset provenance before approving real market history for research.

  • Consistent prices and increasing timestamps
  • Gaps identified for explicit review
  • Source rights and independent dataset approval

Challenge the assumptions

Test an idea. Then question it.

Use approved data to explore strategy signals and offline simulations. Make costs and risk assumptions explicit, and treat every result as evidence to question.

  • Offline, bar-based strategy simulation
  • Explicit trading-cost and risk assumptions
  • Out-of-sample evaluation planned

02 / Capabilities

Purposeful tools.
Clear boundaries.

Build understanding with a small set of tools that make data quality, provenance and research assumptions visible.

01 / Inspect

Market data inspection

Check candle structure, price consistency, timestamps and gaps before a dataset enters research.

02 / Trace

Source provenance

Keep provider records, use rights, dataset checksums and review status attached to the evidence.

03 / Explore

Offline exploration

Explore strategy signals and bar-based simulations with explicit costs and risk assumptions.

Research is the current scope.

The project does not connect to a brokerage for order execution, place trades, or provide a live trading service.
